- **MIT Network**: Cursor, Cognition (founding teams almost entirely MIT)
- **Stanford Network**: Sierra, Physical Intelligence, Contextual (research-heavy)
- **Berkeley Network**: Perplexity, Cursor (lots of Berkeley CS grads)
- **CMU Network**: Harvey, Sierra

### 📊 **Pattern 9: Team Composition by Company Type**

**Research/Foundation Model Companies:**
- PhD-heavy, smaller teams, research scientist titles
- Examples: Physical Intelligence, Contextual

**Developer Tools:**
- Young engineers from elite schools, minimal YoE required
- Examples: Cursor, Cognition

**Enterprise AI:**
- Mix of senior enterprise sales + technical team
- Heavy on "AI Strategist" / Forward Deployed Engineer roles
- Examples: Distyl, Harvey, Glean

**Defense Tech:**
- Domain experts (military/naval), specialized engineering
- Examples: Saronic, Peregrine

### 🚀 **Pattern 10: Scaling Phases**

**Phase 1 (0-10 employees):**
- Founding engineers, mostly from same network
- Heavy on MIT/Stanford/Berkeley

**Phase 2 (10-30 employees):**
- First non-technical hires (recruiter, ops)
- First sales/GTM hire

**Phase 3 (30-100 employees):**
- Enterprise GTM scaling
- Specialized roles (security, legal, finance)
- Management layer emerging

**Cursor's 2025 Scaling Example:**
- Massive GTM hiring (50+ sales roles)
- VP/Director level from Vercel, Figma
- Still hiring senior engineers

## Key Takeaways

1. **School matters less than network**: The strongest pattern is hiring from the same prior company, not just the same school

2. **Scale AI is the #1 feeder**: For AI companies specifically, Scale AI alumni are everywhere (Cognition, Decagon, Sierra, Harvey)

3. **Palantir model persists**: "Forward Deployed Engineer" / "AI Strategist" roles from Palantir alumni in enterprise-focused companies

4. **PhD density correlates with research focus**: Physical Intelligence and Contextual are notably PhD-heavy

5. **Domain expertise matters**: Successful companies embed domain experts (lawyers, police, naval architects) not just engineers

6. **GTM hiring follows clear patterns**: Enterprise SaaS GTM comes from Vercel, Figma, Notion, Slack, Salesforce

7. **Young founders can win**: Cursor's founding team had minimal YoE but succeeded; Cognition's team is very young

8. **Acqui-hires work**: Brainco, Cursor (Supermaven) - acquiring teams from other startups

9. **Eastern European talent**: Perplexity notably taps Russian/Belarusian engineering talent pools

10. **Operations roles unique to robotics**: Physical Intelligence is the only company with significant robot operator roles
